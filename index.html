2 Send to Claude.ai
Article extracted. Copy the prompt below, paste it into Claude.ai chat, wait for response.
↗ Open Claude.ai (new tab)
What to do next:
  1. Click Copy Prompt above
  2. Click Open Claude.ai (opens in new tab)
  3. Paste the prompt in Claude's chat box, press Enter
  4. Wait ~10 seconds for Claude's response
  5. Select Claude's entire response and copy it (Ctrl+A inside the message, then Ctrl+C — or just drag-select the JSON block)
  6. Come back here and paste it into Step 3 below

▸ What's new / version history

v1.15 — Jun 2026

  • Added a "My Projects" button on the landing page so saved projects can be reopened right after loading (previously the library was only reachable from inside a processed article).

v1.14 — Jun 2026

  • Projects. Each processed article is now a "project". New toolbar at the top of Step 4: Save (to a named library on this browser), New, Projects (reopen saved ones), and Download (a self-contained HTML backup you can read anywhere). A silent autosave offers to restore your last project after a crash. Saved projects keep text + cover settings; photos/videos are re-added on reopen.
  • Optional Rephrase. Tick "Also generate a full rephrase" before copying the prompt to get a complete reworded version of the article (your own words, ~same length) — shown as its own copyable section. Off by default.

v1.13 — Jun 2026

  • Fixed video export running longer than the trimmed clip — it now waits for the trim-in point before recording, with a safety timer so a clip can't overrun its length.

v1.12 — Jun 2026

  • Less scrolling after Process Response: a sticky quick-jump bar (Hooks · Cover · Caption · Carousel), the Carousel collapsed by default, and the page auto-scrolls to the results.

v1.11 — Jun 2026

  • Design panel on the cover: pick from 4 layouts (Straddle / Top box / Lower-third / Over image), recolour (primary / accent / text + presets), and add a watermark (page handle text or logo, with position & opacity). Applies to image and video; resets fresh each post.

v1.10 — Jun 2026

  • Clear button to remove the uploaded image/video and return the cover to transparent.
  • Video export now stays in sync with audio (frame-accurate capture).

v1.9 — Jun 2026

  • Video mode for the cover: upload/drag a video, trim up to 60s, zoom & drag to reposition, then Export video (records in real time; MP4 where the browser supports it, else WebM).

v1.8 — Jun 2026

  • Drag & drop an image file straight onto the cover area to upload it — no need to browse your desktop.

v1.7 — Jun 2026

  • New YouTube transcript source: paste a transcript and it auto-strips timestamps and [Music] tags. Includes a best-effort auto-fetch button.
  • Fixed the Manual paste "title is not defined" error.
